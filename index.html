My Non-Linear Path

I don’t fit in boxes. I usually break them to see how they’re made.

My education didn’t happen in a typical classroom. I left an insular community at 15 to find my own way, teaching myself high school to get a GED on public library Wi-Fi. After work as a teacher I would then cycle 20 plus miles to get to night classes at community college. There I learned about chemistry, fell in love with water, and finished my degrees at Cornell University on a full scholarship.

Why does this matter to you?

Because I don’t panic when the blueprint is missing. I’m a self-starter and gritty problem solver.

Spiders are the original systems engineers. They build complex, resilient structures seemingly out of nothing. That is how I approach my work. Whether I’m managing a $350M water infrastructure portfolio, building and running a high-growth startup, or optimizing a commercial real estate firm, I treat every challenge the same way:

  • — Assess the resources
  • — Hack the system
  • — Build a path where there wasn't one

Privacy

Updated November 3, 2026

This is my personal site, run by me, Yitzy Rosenberg. There's no account to create and nothing to buy. Here's what gets collected when you visit, and how to turn it off.

Microsoft Clarity

I use Microsoft Clarity to see how people use this site so I can make it better. It runs on the homepage, Bug Hunt, Books I've Read and the Web Untangle demo, and it captures:

  • Clicks, scrolling, and mouse or finger movement
  • Session recordings and heatmaps of that activity
  • The pages you visit, plus details such as your device, browser and approximate location

Clarity tracks your browser with a pseudonymous ID, using first-party and third-party cookies, and it hides sensitive content from recordings by default.

Microsoft also collects and receives this data, and may use it for its own purposes, including improving its products and providing Microsoft Advertising. The Microsoft Privacy Statement explains how.

Recordings are kept for 30 days. Click and heatmap data, and any session I save, are kept for 9 months.

In the EEA, UK or Switzerland? This site doesn't ask for cookie consent, so Clarity doesn't set cookies for you. It can still collect cookieless data.

Turn Clarity off

This switch stops Clarity from loading in this browser.

JavaScript is off, so Clarity isn't running.

Your choice is saved on this device only, so you'd need to set it again on another device or after clearing your site data. Reload any pages of this site you already have open. A content blocker that blocks clarity.ms works too.

This doesn't delete data that was already collected. Email me if you have a question about that.

Everything else

  • Hosting: this site runs on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log your IP address. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Bug Hunt leaderboard: the global board is stored on Supabase, which receives requests from your browser when the board loads. If you click Submit to Global, the name you enter, your score and your level go on that public board.
  • Books I've Read: cover images load from Open Library, which can see your IP address when it serves them.
  • Saved in your browser: your Bug Hunt scores and settings, whether the spider is on, and your Clarity choice. These stay on your device, apart from any score you submit.
